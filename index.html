01 — Profile

About

A quick spec sheet, and the story behind it.

I'm an Industrial Computer Science & Automation engineering student at INSAT (Tunisia), currently also starting a Research Master's in IoT and Data Analytics at École Polytechnique de Tunisie. My work sits at the intersection of the factory floor and the dashboard: I like taking a messy production or maintenance process and turning it into something measurable, visual, and easier to decide on.

That's shown up concretely at Opella, where I've been building a Power BI dashboard that tracks OEE/TRS across four production lines against a 2025–2030 capacity plan, and a Streamlit-based decision-support tool that helps maintenance teams diagnose air-handling-unit failures against a 45-rule knowledge base. Earlier, at Ulysse Logistics (UTIC Group), I worked on logistics systems optimization.

Outside of internships, I lead technical activities for the IEEE INSAT Student Branch, after a year chairing the IEEE RAS chapter that was named Best IEEE RAS Student Branch Chapter Worldwide under that leadership. I'm now looking for a 4–6 month End-of-Study Internship (PFE) starting between January and April 2027, ideally in production, industrial optimization, or supply chain.
